Six games on the same dimensions

The grid is a heatmap of stance readings. Green, amber, and red mean a positive, mixed, or negative reading that carries at least half the judgments in that cell. A divided cell stays neutral. Fewer than four judgments stay in plain type, without a stance color, because a small sample is not a consensus.
