Frame · dimension graphics

How a dimension reading could be drawn

Two static pages try the radar and the heatmap against Frame’s actual judgments. Each arm of the radar is the share of positive judgments among people who judged that dimension. Each heatmap cell is the stance reading for one work on one dimension, with the sample in the cell. Enjoyment and execution stay off both graphics, because they are separate judgments.

The counts are invented so the shapes have something to show. They are not published evaluations. A marketing sketch of this idea used a 0–10 score per axis. These pages keep positive, mixed, and negative, and they keep the sample visible.
